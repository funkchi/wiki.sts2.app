# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026.

The wiki does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally collect information directly from visitors through forms.

## Hosting and Logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and uses Cloudflare R2 for media. Cloudflare may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser details, timestamps, and security signals to deliver and protect the site.

## Advertising

The site uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted. Visitors can manage Google advertising preferences through [Google's advertising settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

Advertising consent defaults to denied until a visitor makes a choice. Visitors can allow personalized advertising or reject optional advertising signals. The choice is stored only in the visitor's browser and can be changed using the button below.

<button type="button" onclick="window.wikiConsent && window.wikiConsent.open()">Review advertising privacy choices</button>

## Analytics

When enabled for the Pages project, Cloudflare Web Analytics measures page popularity, referrers, navigation type, and performance without using a persistent visitor identifier. The wiki also records first-party events for internal navigation, wiki searches, and searches with no results. These events contain the current path, destination path or search text, and result count. They do not include an IP address, user agent, account identifier, or cross-site identifier in the analytics dataset.

## External Links

The wiki links to external data sources and community resources. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the repository's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/funkchi/wiki.sts2.app/issues).

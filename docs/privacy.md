# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026.

The wiki does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally collect information directly from visitors through forms.

## Hosting and Logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and uses Cloudflare R2 for media. Cloudflare may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser details, timestamps, and security signals to deliver and protect the site.

## Advertising

The site uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted. Visitors can manage Google advertising preferences through [Google's advertising settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

Where consent is legally required, advertising behavior may depend on the consent choices presented to the visitor.

## External Links

The wiki links to external data sources and community resources. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the repository's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/funkchi/wiki.sts2.app/issues).

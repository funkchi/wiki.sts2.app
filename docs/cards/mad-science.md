## Variants

### Attack

Deal 12 damage.
- **Sapping:** Apply 2 Weak. Apply 2 Vulnerable.
- **Violence:** Hits 2 additional times.
- **Choking:** Whenever you play a card this turn, the enemy loses 6 HP.

### Skill

Gain 8 Block.
- **Energized:** Gain 2 Energy.
- **Wisdom:** Draw 3 cards.
- **Chaos:** Add a random card into your Hand. It's free to play this turn.

### Power

No description available.
- **Expertise:** Gain 2 Strength. Gain 2 Dexterity.
- **Curious:** Powers cost 1 1 Energy less.

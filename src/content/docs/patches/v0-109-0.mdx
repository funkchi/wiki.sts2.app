**Released July 17, 2026 (beta branch).** Official notes from Mega Crit, annotated with links to this wiki's card, relic, enemy, and event pages. Source: [Steam announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2868840/view/711155348639056490).

Previous patch: [v0.108.0 — Beta Patch](/patches/v0-108-0/). Next patch: [v0.110.0 — Beta Patch](/patches/v0-110-0/).

This patch brings a host of balance changes, bug fixes, and new Epoch art, plus the start of Traditional Chinese localization. Most notably, two new Neow relics arrive — one granting a powerful exclusive potion, the other a brand new quest card.

Card and relic pages on this wiki are still showing pre-v0.108.0 values — the upstream data source has not yet ingested either patch. Treat the values in these notes as authoritative until the entity pages catch up.

## Hotfix v0.109.1

**Released July 25, 2026.** A small follow-up patch with a single bug fix, from its own [Steam announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2868840/allnews/):

- Synced corrected Traditional Chinese translations to fix broken plural evaluation

## Content & Balance

### Ancients

- Added new **Neow** relic, **Dowsing Rod** — "Upon pickup, add 1 Dowsing to your Deck."
  - **Dowsing** — Quest - 0 cost - "After entering 5 more ? rooms, transform this into Abundance."
  - **Abundance** — Ancient - Skill - Cost 1 - "Choose 1 of 3 (Upgraded) Powers to add into your Hand. It is free to play this turn. Exhaust."
- Added new **Neow** relic, **Neow's Sacrifice** — "Upon pickup, procure 1 Ambergris and add 1 [Guilty](/docs/cards/guilty/) to your Deck."
  - **Ambergris** potion — "Heal for 50% of your max HP. Take an extra turn after this one."
- Reworked Nonupeipe's [Diamond Diadem](/docs/relics/diamond-diadem/) relic: "Whenever you play 2 or fewer cards in a turn, take half damage from enemies." → "Start combat with 20 Block. Block is not removed at the start of your next turn."
- Moved Orobos' [Sand Castle](/docs/relics/sand-castle/) relic from Pool 1 → Pool 2 (second option instead of first option)
- Changed up Darv blessing pools for more variety (you will see Energy relics more often)
- Nerfed Tanx's [Meat Cleaver](/docs/relics/meat-cleaver/) relic: HP gain decreased from 9 → 5
- Buffed Tezcatara's [Toy Box](/docs/relics/toy-box/) relic: relics gained increased from 4 → 5
- Buffed Vakuu's [Fiddle](/docs/relics/fiddle/) relic: card draw increased from 2 → 3
- Changed Vakuu's [Sere Talon](/docs/relics/sere-talon/) and [Distinguished Cape](/docs/relics/distinguished-cape/) relics: swapped downsides

### Enemies

- Nerfed [Aeonglass](/docs/enemies/aeonglass/): Ebb move damage decreased from 26(32) → 22(26) to lower its front-loaded damage
- Buffed [Torch Head Amalgam](/docs/enemies/torch-head-amalgam/): now starts with a new Strong Tackle move on turn 1, increasing front-loaded damage from 18(22) → 26(32)

### Events

- Nerfed [History Course](/docs/relics/history-course/) relic: now only repeats Attacks, does not affect Skills

### Ironclad

- Buffed [Demon Form](/docs/cards/demon-form/): Strength gain increased from 2(3) → 3(4)
- Buffed [Expect a Fight](/docs/cards/expect-a-fight/): no longer says "You cannot gain additional Energy this turn."
- Buffed [Primal Force](/docs/cards/primal-force/): [Giant Rock](/docs/cards/giant-rock/) token damage increased from 16(20) → 20(24)
- Changed [Taunt](/docs/cards/taunt/):
  - Block gain decreased from 7(8) → 6(7)
  - Rarity decreased from Uncommon → Common
- Changed [Bloodletting](/docs/cards/bloodletting/): rarity increased from Common → Uncommon
- Changed [Cruelty](/docs/cards/cruelty/): rarity decreased from Rare → Uncommon
- Changed [Dominate](/docs/cards/dominate/): rarity increased from Uncommon → Rare

### Silent

- Reworked [Mirage](/docs/cards/mirage/): Uncommon - Skill - 1(0) - "Gain Block equal to Poison on ALL enemies. Exhaust." → Uncommon - Skill - 0 - "Next turn, gain 1(2) Energy."
- Reworked [Well-Laid Plans](/docs/cards/well-laid-plans/):
  - Uncommon - Power - Cost 1 - "At the end of your turn, Retain up to 1(2) card(s)." → Rare - Power - Cost 1(0) - "At the end of your turn, you no longer discard your Hand."
  - Now also available in multiplayer, was previously singleplayer-only
- Reworked [Expertise](/docs/cards/expertise/): "Draw cards until you have 6(7) in your hand." → "Draw 2(3) cards. They gain Retain this turn."
- Buffed [Outbreak](/docs/cards/outbreak/): damage increased from 3(4) → 4(5)
- Changed [Accelerant](/docs/cards/accelerant/): rarity decreased from Rare → Uncommon

### Regent

- Reworked [Pillar of Creation](/docs/cards/pillar-of-creation/): "Whenever you create a card, gain 3 Block." → "The first time you create a card each turn, gain 5(7) Block."
- Nerfed [Collision Course](/docs/cards/collision-course/): damage decreased from 11(15) → 10(14)

### Necrobinder

- Reworked [Eidolon](/docs/cards/eidolon/): "Exhaust your Hand. If 9 cards were Exhausted this way, gain 1 Intangible." → "Play ALL Ethereal cards in your Exhaust Pile. Exhaust."

### Defect

- Buffed [Hyperbeam](/docs/cards/hyperbeam/): damage increased from 28(36) → 30(38)
- Buffed [Sunder](/docs/cards/sunder/): damage increased from 24(32) → 26(34)
- Changed [Trash to Treasure](/docs/cards/trash-to-treasure/): upgrade changed from gaining Innate → lowering cost by 1

### Multiplayer

- Added new **Regent** card, **Tutor**: Rare - Skill - 1(0) - "Another player chooses a card in their Draw Pile to add into their Hand."
- Nerfed **Midnight**: damage decreased from 99(120) → 60(72)
- Nerfed **The Ball**: scaling decreased from +15(25) → +10(15)
- Nerfed **Blade Symphony**:
  - Cost increased from 1 → 2(1)
  - Upgrade no longer increases Shiv count

## User Experience & Interface

### General

- Seeds are now 12 characters long and the internal seed is larger
- Removed faint white outline from game cursor

### Multiplayer

- You can now play nearly any potion on another player
- Map ink circles are now the same for all players

## Art

### General

- Added asset for **Ambergris** potion
- Added beta portrait art for **Dowsing** and **Abundance** cards

### Enemies

- Swapped [Aeonglass](/docs/enemies/aeonglass/) double and heavy attack animations and improved the double attack VFX

### Multiplayer

## Writing

### Localization

- Added Traditional Chinese (zht) as a selectable language with English fallback
- Added Traditional Chinese fonts (Source Han Serif TC, Noto Sans Mono CJK TC)
- Updated translations for various languages
- Fixed translation errors for French, Italian, Russian, and Thai
- Removed stale translations for redesigned Well-Laid Plans, [Fuel](/docs/cards/fuel/), Outbreak, Sere Talon, and Cacophony

## Bug Fixes

### General

- VSync setting in Graphics options now shows the correct On/Off state after restarting
- "Power wears off" notification no longer blocks mouse input
- Placeholder Orb numbers no longer briefly show up at the start of combat
- Correctly snapped scrolling menus at low FPS

### Ancients

- Fixed [Tanx's Whistle](/docs/relics/tanxs-whistle/) card preview and tooltip snapping to the top-left corner when hovered

### Enemies

- Fixed awkward delay when [Osty](/docs/enemies/osty/) and Kaiser Krab would spawn at start of combat
- Fixed [Fabricator](/docs/enemies/fabricator/) animations that weren't playing right
- Fixed [Fat Gremlin](/docs/enemies/fat-gremlin/) sometimes spawning without a bag of money when it should

### Silent

- [Expose](/docs/cards/expose/) card now properly triggers [Hand Drill](/docs/relics/hand-drill/) relic

### Necrobinder

- **Soulbound** card now shuffles souls into the Draw Pile instead of putting them on the bottom

### Multiplayer

- **One For All** card no longer previews damage up on X-cost cards
- Fixed [Unmovable](/docs/cards/unmovable/) card causing other players to receive double Block from [Demonic Shield](/docs/cards/demonic-shield/) card when it was not the first Block card played
- **Imitation Learning** card now clones cards before they are played instead of after, allowing enchantments like Swift to apply to the cloning player
- **Underworld** card no longer causes Doom to be applied to players when enemies deal attack damage
- Fixed an issue where, if a player played **The Ball** while another player had the [History Course](/docs/relics/history-course/) relic, History Course could incorrectly end up playing a copy of The Ball next turn
- Fixed divergence that would sometimes occur when ending combat with **The Ball**
- **Hibernate** card no longer gives Block to dead players
- **Hibernate** card now correctly displays stacks in its power icon
- If [Huddle Up](/docs/cards/huddle-up/) triggers a choice for another player, the playing player is no longer blocked from playing cards
- **The Ball** now triggers the playing player's relics, not the receiving player's
- Fixed card effects breaking when a player dies while their own card is still resolving (e.g. to Thorns)
- Fixed divergence that would occur if one player took a long time to enter combat
- Player icons are rendered above the ink circle on the map
- Resolved issue where, if Epochs were deprecated, that player could no longer join multiplayer games

## Modding

- Merged `SavedPropertySerializationCache` into `ModelIdSerializationCache`. SavedProperties are now included in its hash.
- Models from non-gameplay-affecting mods are now properly excluded in the hash
